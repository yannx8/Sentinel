Sentinel design system

Calm, modern and smooth. One brand colour, role-based radii, glyph plus label for every status, and one memorable element: the Thread. This page renders the tokens from docs/DESIGN_SYSTEM.md.

Colour

Named by role. Every text pairing meets 4.5:1 and every control or glyph meets 3:1, checked by script.

Typography

Atkinson Hyperlegible Next carries the interface. Bricolage Grotesque gives titles and figures a voice. If the fonts do not load, a system fallback shows.
